
# Business Recommendations – Customer Churn Analysis

## 1. Encourage Long-Term Contracts

**Finding:** Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 42.71%. The churn rates for one-year and two-year contracts are 11.27% and 2.83%, respectively.

**Recommendation:** Offer suitable discounts or benefits to encourage month-to-month customers to consider long-term contracts. Monitor churn rates to evaluate whether these offers are effective.

## 2. Improve the Payment Experience

**Finding:** Customers who use electronic checks have the highest churn rate at 45.29%. The churn rates for mailed checks, automatic bank transfers, and automatic credit card payments are 19.11%, 16.71%, and 15.24%, respectively.

**Recommendation:** Investigate whether customers experience difficulties with the electronic check payment process. Explain convenient payment options, including automatic payments, and monitor whether customer retention improves.

## 3. Improve the Early Customer Experience

**Finding:** Customers who churn have an average tenure of 17.98 months, compared with 37.57 months for customers who do not churn. Churned customers also have higher average monthly charges (74.44) than retained customers (61.27).

**Recommendation:** Provide early support to new customers, explain plan charges clearly, and offer suitable plans based on customer needs. Contact customers who show signs of dissatisfaction and evaluate whether these actions reduce churn.

## Conclusion

The SQL analysis shows that churn is associated with contract type, payment method, customer tenure, and monthly charges. The business should test targeted retention offers, improve the payment experience, and strengthen early customer support. The results should be monitored to determine whether these actions reduce churn.

**Note:** These findings show associations in the dataset. They do not prove that contract type, payment method, or monthly charges directly cause customer churn.
